title: "Compatible Mods"

The following is a list of mods we include official compatibility for. Mods not listed here may or may not work.

### Chat Styling Mods

* [Simple Chat Fork](https://modrinth.com/mod/simplechatfork/)
* [Beautified Chat (Server)](https://modrinth.com/mod/beautified-chat-server)
* [StyledChat](https://modrinth.com/mod/styled-chat)

* [Fabric Tailor](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabrictailor)
* [Maintenance Mode](https://modrinth.com/mod/maintenancemode)
* [FTB Ranks (Forge/NeoForge)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ftb-ranks-forge)
* [FTB Ranks (Fabric)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ftb-ranks-fabric)

* [Player Revive Mod](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/playerrevive)
