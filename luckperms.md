Maintenance mode commands now support LuckPerms integration. Below is the list of commands, and their luck perms permissions.

Command | LuckPerm Permission
--- | ---
maintenance on | maintenance.enable
maintenance off | maintenance.disable
maintenance status | maintenance.status
maintenance reload | maintenance.reload
maintenance setMessage | maintenance.setmessage
maintenance setMotd | maintenance.setmotd
|
maintenance addAllowed | maintenance.addUser
maintenance removeAllowed | maintenance.removeUser
maintenance list | maintenance.list
|
maintenance backup | maintenance.backup
maintenance doBackups | maintenance.setbackups
|
maintenance schedule | maintenance.schedule
maintenance schedule disableOnRestart | maintenance.changerestart
maintenance schedule untilRestart | maintenance.untilrestart
maintenance repeating start | maintenance.startschedule
maintenance repeating end | maintenance.endschedule
