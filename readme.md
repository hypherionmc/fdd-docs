A simple Rich Presence Mod for Stardew Valley
&nbsp; &nbsp;

![](https://cdn.firstdark.dev/artpacks/srpc-stardew/srpc_information.png)

Simple Discord RPC is a simple and easy to use mod that allows you to display what you are doing in Stardew Valley on your Discord Status. This is useful for modpacks that want to advertise themselves, or for people who simply want a nicer way to show that they are playing Stardew Valley.

This mod works out of the box, but you can customize it to suit your needs. Please refer to the WIKI above for more info

![](https://cdn.firstdark.dev/artpacks/srpc-stardew/srpc_help.png)

You can always refer to the [WIKI](https://rpc-stardew.fdd-docs.com) for help, but while I try to keep everything as simple as possible and keep the docs as useful as possible, sometimes things go wrong or it just doesn't make any sense.

You are more than welcome to open an Issue on GitHub if it's a bug or suggestion, or for quicker support, you can join our discord and ask your question in the appropriate channel

![](https://cdn.firstdark.dev/artpacks/srpc-stardew/srpc_permissions.png)
